# Krishti AI V14.2 — Complete Login & OTP Fix

## Authentication
- Firebase Email/Password login and account creation wired cleanly.
- Google popup login preserved.
- Phone authentication rebuilt with country-code selector, phone normalization, Firebase reCAPTCHA lifecycle reset, OTP verification, resend countdown and error handling.
- Phone input accepts national digits and automatically prefixes the selected country code.
- OTP input is numeric and limited to 6 digits.
- reCAPTCHA is cleared after failed/successful OTP flows to avoid stale verifier issues.
- Logout resets phone-auth state.

## Important Firebase setup
Firebase Authentication must have Email/Password, Phone and Google enabled. The deployed website domain must also be listed under Authentication -> Settings -> Authorized domains. Phone Auth uses Firebase's SMS quota and can be limited on the Spark plan.

## Limitation
The app cannot make an incorrect email/password valid. If an email account was created only with Google, use Google login or create an Email/Password account with that email after Firebase permits it.
